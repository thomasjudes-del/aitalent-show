What we want

Beautiful.
Funny. Useful. Unhinged.

We are looking for work that makes people stop, watch, laugh, admire or ask: “wait, how did you make that?”

Beautiful

A simple idea becomes unexpectedly moving, elegant or cinematic.

Funny

A bizarre, clever or absurd act that people instantly want to share.

Useful

An AI tool, workflow or agent that solves a real problem sharply.

Beautiful failure

A glorious mistake or happy accident that becomes the whole point.
